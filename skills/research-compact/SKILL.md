# Research Compact

Adapted from ECC `strategic-compact` at
`7a5757e6c0d7e8e1080d30169b4b044d76e0f7fc`. The local form is manual and
scientific; no lifecycle hook or automatic context injection is installed.

## Build the compact

Capture:

- the research question, scope, terminology, and current hypotheses;
- search surfaces, coverage, strongest evidence, contradictory evidence, and exclusions;
- datasets, environments, experiment status, raw-result locations, and recorded verdicts;
- decisions already made and the evidence or project authority behind them;
- unresolved uncertainty, risks, and the next reproducible action;
- suggested skills for the next session.

Reference existing artifacts instead of duplicating them: link specs, research notes,
citations, datasets, result files, commits, issues, and decisions by stable path or URL.
Redact credentials, personally identifiable information, participant data, unpublished
restricted results, and other secrets. Preserve uncertainty rather than compressing it into
false certainty.

## Completion gate

The compact is complete when a fresh session can locate the primary artifacts, distinguish
verified evidence from inference and unreviewed memory, understand the present state, and
perform the next action without reconstructing prior work.
